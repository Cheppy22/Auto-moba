# Cards worth playing (2026-10-14)

Owner-approved follow-up to [BALANCE-2026-10-13.md](BALANCE-2026-10-13.md) "The AI uses the pawn plan": with the AI on the pawn plan, a Tempo spent on a card was worth less than a Tempo spent on a pawn. Fix direction: make cards cheaper and stronger, don't weaken pawns, then let the AI play cards again. Branch `test-product-2`, Long Board unless named. Rules in [CHESS.md](CHESS.md) "Gambits"; linked from [SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md](SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md).

## BLUF

- **Cards cost about a third less** (every cost ×⅔, rounded; order kept; cheapest 7, dearest 30, was 10–45).
- **Every card's effect is half again as strong** (the bonus part ×1.5, one multiplier for every school; durations unchanged).
- **The AI plays its best card whenever it keeps 40 Tempo after paying** (`pawns.aiCardReserve` 20 → 40), lanes full or not. The "cards only while every lane is full" rule is gone. The pawn plan's own 40 reserve is unchanged.
- **Results:** idle player 54.3% → 49.5%; card-a-minute player 43.4% → 54.5%; AI vs AI White 53.1% (800); the AI plays about 6–7 cards per Act (was 1–2), its first at about 50 s (was about 200 s); AI-vs-AI p90 25.3 → 22.2 min (median 14.5). Throne and respawn were not touched.

## Not changed

- Pawns (cost 15, stats, cap), the pawn plan (2 / 2 / 2, reserve 40), start Tempo 30, income, Throne and respawn, the AI's choice of card (`aiCardPick`).

## Open choices

- **Card player at 54.5% (target 55–60%).** More card strength did not move it (×2 gave 50.0%) because the AI plays about 7 cards per Act against the stand-in's 4. If the owner wants a clearer reward for playing cards, the lever is how many cards the AI plays, not their strength: a higher `pawns.aiCardReserve` (for example 45–50) means fewer AI cards, which lifts both player rows (idle has room up to 52%) but takes AI cards below about 6 per Act.
- **Idle player matches stay long** (p90 about 25 min): a player who never plays cards fields about 82 pawns and lanes stall. AI vs AI is 22.2 min, so Throne and respawn were left alone.
- **Relic card discounts** (Sealed Move −10, Opening Theory ±5) were not scaled down with the cheaper cards; they are relatively stronger now.

### Phase 0: the hoarding, confirmed

Current build before the armory (200 AI-vs-AI matches; per piece, both sides):

| --------------- | ------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------ |

| 3               | 109     | 2,626 (3,953)             | 2.1%                       | 4                  |
| 4               | 47      | 4,390 (6,148)             | 4.9%                       | 5                  |

The spec's 12-match numbers (411 / 924 / 2,503 / 4,109; 2 of 60 pieces with a Masterwork by Act 3) hold.

**Why (measured in 4 matches, a piece's state read once a second):** once a piece owns the parts of its next build item, a Masterwork, it stops buying at base (`nextPurchase` saves for the jungle Square) and can buy it only on a shop trip. In 52% of the seconds a piece had 700+ gold, its next buy was an affordable Masterwork, but **91% of those seconds the trip was blocked** by the "no enemy within 420" check in `strategicUpdate` (`src/sim/ai/strategic.ts`, the `visitShop` candidate): it counts enemy pawnlings, and a lane piece almost always has some near. In the remaining 9% the trip lost the goal scoring to pushing or farming (it won 13 of 526 times). Result: about 0.5 Masterworks bought per match, and gold piles up all match.

**AI bug that also hurts live play:** yes. The player's pieces shop with the same AI during Acts, so they hoard the same way. Proposed fix (balance-changing, so left for phase 3): count only enemy **pieces** in that check (or allow a couple of pawnlings). With the armory most of the hoard is now spent at the break anyway, so the fix matters less than before.
