## BLUF

At every Act break the break sheet gains a third tab, **Relics**: you see three relics and keep one for the rest of the match (up to 3 held). Most relics are **bound** when you take them: to a lane, a piece, a pawn quota or a gambit plus a trigger. At every later break you may re-aim every relic you hold for free. One of the three offers is **tailored** to what went wrong in the Act report and arrives pre-aimed at the problem (a lane that bled pawnlings offers a lane relic already set to that lane). This makes the break the moment you decide the plan (which lane to push, which piece carries, how Tempo is spent, which school you lean on) and lets the live round play it out. Several relics also turn live taps into standing rules set at the break (auto-fielding pawns, a gambit that fires on its own trigger, a lane card that is always on), so the live gambit hand can shrink later.

## Interactions

- **Orders.** Lane relics (Half-Open File, Closed Position, Wing Gambit, Strong Square, Pawn Lever, Standing Order) are aimed on the Orders tab's lane rows, so lanes and their doctrine are planned together. Battery is the only relic that breaks 2/2/1; Orders and the AI re-plan must accept 3/1/1 while it is held. Black's re-plan (75% chance) also re-aims its lane relics to the lane it moves pieces to.
- **Openings.** Sealed Move and Opening Theory read the Opening's schools; Standing Order takes a card out of the deck, so the Opening's weights apply to fewer cards. English (wave 2) gets Clock cards into Sealed Move with no extra work.
- **Gambit schools.** Every relic counter above goes through the existing school counters or piece styles; no relic blocks or cancels an enemy card or relic (owner rule). Relic and card effects stack normally.
- **Rank 4 archetypes.** Piece relics are taken before or after Rank 4 and follow the piece through its style pick. The AI's Rank 4 pick should treat a Crown Jewel or Development piece as Offense. Development moves fork pauses earlier. Pawn Chain and Pawn Majority pair with the five pawn-aura styles (Warlord, Bastion, Light, Sovereign, Paladin); the balance run should check they are not mandatory for those styles.
- **Check and Checkmate.** King's Ransom softens Check; Royal Keep softens the Throne's Endgame weakness. Neither stops Checkmate; both delay it, so watch match length.

## Tuning targets and tests

- **White win rate** stays 48–52% with both sides drafting by AI; median match stays 12–16 min.
- **Relic value:** White drafting by AI rule vs White with none: +6–12 win-rate points (they matter, but less than the whole gambit system).
- **Choice matters:** AI-rule picks vs random picks (both aimed by the AI): at least +3 points. If not, relics are flavour and need bigger numbers.
- **Per relic:** win rate when held within 46–56%; AI pick rate when offered 15–60%. Outliers get one number changed, then re-run.
- **Comeback:** the side behind after Act 2 wins 25–35% (should rise a few points with Rally relics, never above 40%).
- **Runs:** `npm run balance -- --matches 800 --seed 29000 --quiet` with `--relics A=ai,B=ai`, then `A=none`, then `A=random`; one `--relic-pool` run per family.
- **Tests (light):** one unit test per relic effect; same seed gives the same offers and picks; a headless break resolves both picks; one Playwright test: open the Relics tab, pick, aim, resume.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-10, all as recommended)

1. **Name clash:** tier 3 items are already called **Relics** (ITEMS.md). Recommend: keep "Relics" for the draft (the bigger new system) and rename the item tier to **Masterwork** (labels only, no rule change).
2. **A draft at setup too?** Recommend **no** for now: setup already has the Opening, five paths and lanes, and Act 1 is when you learn Black's plan. Revisit if 2–3 relics a match feels thin.
3. **Re-aim only at breaks, or also during Adjourn?** Recommend **breaks only**, so the break stays the planning moment; Adjourn keeps lanes and paths as now.
4. **Must you pick?** Recommend **yes**, with "Let the AI choose", same as forks. A skip option is almost never right and adds a decision with no plan behind it.
5. **Which to build first?** Recommend a first wave of 8 Low/Med relics that cover all five families (Half-Open File, Closed Position, Crown Jewel, Development, Pawn Majority, Pawn Lever, Opening Theory, Standing Order), then Sealed Move and Pawn Structure, then the High ones (Battery, Sortie).

## Appendix: trimming the live hand later (recommendation only)

- Once Standing Order exists, move the four lane-buff cards (Advance, Hold the File, Open File, Pawn Storm) to set-at-the-break form only; the hand keeps signature, Initiative and Sacrifice cards.
- Cut the hand from 3 to 2 cards; Quiet Move is the test bed for this before it becomes the default.
- Slow the churn: refill 6 → 10 s and expiry 40 → 60 s, so the hand changes less and asks for fewer glances.
- Keep Field Pawn; Pawn Structure covers players who don't want to tap it. Since 2026-10-11 every White side also has a base standing pawn plan (CHESS.md "Pawnlings and pawns"); Pawn Structure is the better version of it (a chosen reserve, and auto pawns cost 2 less) and takes over while it is held.
- Lower the gambit-value target from 8–15 to 4–8 points once relics carry the planning, and check it with taps per minute in a browser playtest.

## Wave 1 build notes (2026-10-10)

Code: `content/relics.json` (+ `RelicSchema` in `src/sim/content/schema.ts`, `tuning.json` → `relics`), `src/sim/relics.ts` (effects: stat mods through `recompute`, Pawn Majority cost and cap, Opening Theory card cost, Development bounty, Pawn Lever structure damage), `src/sim/draft.ts` (offers, picks, AI scoring and aiming), `src/sim/callouts.ts` (coach rules, moved from `src/analysis` so the sim can read them), UI in `src/ui/relics.tsx`, `src/ui/hud/RelicsTab.tsx`, `src/ui/hud/RelicRow.tsx`. Commands: `pickRelic`, `declineRelic`, `autoRelic`, `aimRelic`. Events: `relicOffered`, `relicTaken`, `relicAimed`, `relicDeclined`. Config: `MatchConfig.relics` (`ai`, `none`, `random`, `manual`); balance flags `--relics A=ai|none|random,B=...` and `--relic-pool <lane|carry|pawns|siege|orders>`.

Deviations from the design above (all small; the numbers are the design's):

- **Tailored slot with only wave 1 relics.** The family comes from the worst callout that is not a win (so `carry-damage` counts, and `fights-lost-<lane>` offers Lane Doctrine aimed at that lane, `damage-behind` Carry & Court). If the mapped family has no relic left to offer, slot 1 is a random family and has no "why" line. Callouts with no mapping (obelisks) also fall back to random. Wins (`clean`, `bastion-taken`) are used only when there is no problem to answer.
- **Rally relics.** Only Closed Position exists in wave 1, and it belongs to Lane Doctrine. When the side is behind (a fallen Throne, fewer Bastions standing, or 40+ less Tempo earned in the Act) the tailored slot is a Rally relic, so a lost Bastion offers Closed Position aimed at that lane rather than Pawn Lever. "Behind" is read once, when the break starts, and holds for the whole next Act; a Rally effect is off in an Act that starts while the side is not behind.
- **Aiming from Orders.** Lane relics are aimed on the Relics tab only (lane chips L/M/R); the Orders lane board has no relic chips yet.
- **Tailored aim only when it fits the relic** (2026-10-10). A problem callout (bad tone: `died-<piece>`, `fell-<piece>`, `pawnlings-<lane>`, `fights-lost-<lane>`, `bastion-lost`) aims only a defensive or protecting relic (Closed Position, a Fortress Standing Order, later Guarded Piece). A strength (`bastion-taken`, `carry-damage`) aims only an offensive or carry relic. Otherwise the AI rule aims it (best lane or top-damage piece for offence, worst lane or most-died piece for defence). Before this fix, wave 1 put Crown Jewel or Development on the piece that died most, and Half-Open File on the losing lane. New relics must use `aim: protect` or `aim: defense` to take a problem hint.
- **AI aiming at later breaks.** AI sides (Black, and White in headless runs) re-aim their lane relics at every break to the lane the report favours (best lane for offence, worst for defence; Standing Order keeps its card). The design tied it to the 75% lane re-plan move; this is simpler and always on. Piece relics stay where they were taken.
- **Standing Order** is 40% strength (half before the 2026-10-11 pass) through stat mods: Advance +4% move speed, Hold the File +6% armor and resist on pieces, Open File +10% move speed, Pawn Storm +16.7% pawn and pawnling damage and speed. It does not issue Advance's push order. The bound card leaves the deck, and is dealt again if it sat in the hand.
- **Closed Position** weakens pawnlings, not elite pawns. **Pawn Lever** also boosts a Pawn Sacrifice hit (it is still outside the 9%/s cap).
- **Full hand.** "Let the AI choose" with three held replaces the AI's weakest relic if the best offer scores higher, otherwise declines. Random mode replaces a random relic or declines with equal chance.
- **Offers for every side, every break.** Both sides' offers are drawn (White first) even when a side's mode is `none`, so runs with different relic modes stay comparable.
- **Bounds.** Lane move-speed bonuses (tag `laneSpeed`: Half-Open File, Standing Order Advance and Open File, the Advance and Open File cards) multiply and stop at +30% in total (`tuning.relics.laneSpeedCap`); relic pawn-cap bonuses stop at +2 (`pawnCapMax`).
- **Not run:** the 800-match tuning runs in "Tuning targets and tests". Wave 1 numbers are untested beyond a 40-match sanity run.

## Wave 2 build notes (2026-10-11)

All 15 remaining relics, with the numbers as written above (data in `content/relics.json`; Sealed Move's trigger to card pairs for the AI are in its `triggerCards`). Code: effects through `recompute` in `src/sim/relics.ts` (Wing Gambit, Curtain Wall, Royal Keep, Battery's lone-piece armor), lookups in the same file (Tempo cap and rate, hand size and refill, Throne damage, Check penalty, camp damage, Wing melee, `formationError`), and the live part in `src/sim/relicsLive.ts` (a tick that runs every 5, 10 and 20 ticks; Act-start setup; Sealed Move, Pawn Structure, Strong Square, Sortie, Pawn Chain, Curtain Wall regen, Guarded Piece, King's Ransom, Knight's Tour). `src/sim/draft.ts` aims Sealed Move and Pawn Structure for the AI, checks White's picks and fits lanes for Battery. New events: `sealedMove` (fired or lapsed) and `relicTriggered` (Sortie, a toppled banner). New runtime state `RelicState.run`. Held relics carry `trigger`, `quota` and `reserve` besides lane, piece and card.

Aim kinds (the bias fix still holds: only `protect` and `defense` relics take a problem hint): Guarded Piece `protect`; Wing Gambit, Strong Square `defense` (the lane that is already losing is the one you concede or hold); Battery `offense` (best lane); Curtain Wall, Royal Keep, Sortie `defense`; the rest offence. Sealed Move and Pawn Structure ignore hints and are set by the AI rule below.

Deviations and choices:

- **Stat paths.** Conditions that follow the board (Guarded Piece within 300, King's Ransom in Check, Knight's Tour out of combat, Strong Square's aura, Pawn Chain) are modifiers that a 5 or 10 tick pass keeps alive for 12 or 24 ticks, like Outpost's aura, so they show in the Mit column and obey the stat floors. They lag a condition by under half a second.
- **Strong Square.** The "river crossing" is the lane's midpoint (progress 0.5), the same place Open File marks. The banner is a standing Outpost-style aura (it shows as one on the board). "Alone" means exactly one enemy piece within 160 and none of yours; it topples after 5 s of that and stays down until the next Act.
- **Sealed Move.** The trigger is checked once a second; it is level-triggered ("a Bastion of yours under 50%" skips Bastions that were already under 50% when the Act started). Targeting is the AI gambit `plan` for the card (best-fitting target, ignoring its usual score floor); no valid target waits like short Tempo. Once tripped it waits up to 5 s for Tempo and a target, then lapses and the Act's shot is spent. Cost is the card's cost (Opening Theory counts) minus 10. The card stays in the deck. The trigger "Crown Jewel or Guarded piece under 40%" can be picked only while one of those relics is held. The AI picks the trigger its Opening and report favour (Fortress or Position and a lost Bastion: Bastion; Initiative or Sacrifice: kill; Position: 3+ in a lane; March: clock) and the first card of that trigger's list that its Opening allows.
- **Pawn Structure.** Checked every half second; one pawn per check, to the lane furthest under quota (ties Left, Mid, Right). Quotas may total at most the pawn cap of the next Act. Auto pawns cost 2 less (never below 0). The AI sets 6 pawns (3/2/1, most to its best lane) and a reserve of 20, and re-sets them at each break. Black's own Tempo heuristic still fields pawns too; both share the cap.
- **Spare Tempo, Compensation.** The Act-start bonus is Tempo earned, so it counts in the Act report. Compensation's trickle also counts as earned.
- **Quiet Move.** The hand shrinks or grows at the next Act start (a break always sits between pick and start). The HUD hand shows 2 columns while it is held.
- **Battery.** Taking it (or aiming it, for White or the AI) moves pieces for you: the bound lane gets 3, the others 1, taking attackers first and leaving defenders alone in the lone lanes. Replacing it moves them back to 2/1/2. Orders let White fill the bound lane to 3; the sim does not check lanes as pieces move one at a time, but **Resume is refused** (button locked with the reason) until the lanes are 2/1/2 or, while Battery is held, 3/1/1 with the 3 in its lane. Black keeps Battery on its first lane (no re-aim). No lane has a jungler in a three-piece lane or in a lone-piece lane (existing rule: only a two-piece lane has one).
- **Sortie.** "First time each Bastion drops below 50%" is once per Bastion per match, counted only while the relic works (while behind). The rotator gets a `defend` order for 20 s (the Hold the File hook); pieces assigned to that lane and the rotator get +15% blade and soul damage for 12 s.
