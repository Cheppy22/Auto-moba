# Balance pass (2026-10-11)

Overnight balance pass on the chess variant (branch `test-product-2`). Linked from [SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md](SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md) "Balance targets and method". Targets are in [CHESS.md](CHESS.md) "Balance targets" and [RELICS.md](RELICS.md) "Tuning targets and tests".

## BLUF

- **White/Black bias: root cause found and fixed.** The Mid pawnling wave of the "leading" team spawned 292 units ahead, and the leader alternated by wave number starting with **Black on wave 1** of every match. Black's first Mid wave reached the clash first, so Black led after Act 1 in about 3 of 4 matches. `waves.midLeadUnits` is now 0 (both Mid waves start level, like the side lanes).
- Measured: White 49.6% with relics off (800 matches, seed 29000; before 45.8%), median 14.3 min.

## Method

- Full matches: `npm run balance -- --matches N --seed S --quiet --workers 2` with `--relics A=none,B=none` (relics off) or `A=ai,B=ai` (both draft). `--tag NAME` (new) keeps runs from overwriting each other's `out/` files. Long runs were streamed (one `tools/balance-worker.ts` per worker writing one JSON line per match) and merged with `tools/merge-runs.ts` (new), so a run can be read before it ends.
- Act 1 probe (diagnosis only, not committed): the same AI-vs-AI match stepped for one Act (4800 ticks), relics off, recording each side's net worth. Black minus White net worth at the end of Act 1 is a continuous number, so it shows a bias with about 100 matches instead of 800.
- The machine was shared with browser tests (load 25–45 on 4 cores); runs were capped at 2 worker processes, one at a time.

## Root cause: the Mid wave stagger

`spawnWaves` (`src/sim/systems.ts`) spawns every lane's wave for both teams at the base. To stop the short Mid lane from clashing at the exact centre every wave, one team's Mid wave spawned `waves.midLeadUnits` (292.5 on the 1950 map, about 8 s of walking) up the lane. The leader alternated by wave number: `waveNo % 2 === 0 ? 'A' : 'B'`, and the first wave of a match has `waveNo` 1, so **Black always led the first Mid wave**. That first clash decided early last hits, the first Mid trade and the first Tempo, and the lead snowballed: Black was ahead in net worth after Act 1 in 73% of matches, ahead in all three lanes (the Mid piece rotates), and White won about 45%.

Checks that ruled out other causes: the map, bases, towers, slots, obelisks and keeper spots mirror across y = x (`tests/symmetry.test.ts`); jungle shops are point-mirrored but both teams' lane plans are drawn the same way; gambits off kept the bias; reversing the unit update order roughly halved it while the stagger was in place but changed nothing once the stagger was gone.

Fix: `waves.midLeadUnits` 292.5 → 0 in `content/tuning.json` (numbers only; the alternation code stays but has no effect). Both Mid waves now start level and meet at the centre, as the side lanes always did.

## Relics

### Why the AI's picks were lopsided

The AI scores each offer: base 1, +1.0 for the tailored offer when its worst bad callout has severity ≥ 0.6 (nearly every break), +0.5 per matching tag, + jitter 0–0.4. Three things skewed it:

1. **The tailored bonus was decisive.** A trailing side's tailored slot is a Rally relic; with a lost Bastion the family is Siege, whose only Rally relic is Sortie, so Sortie was offered most often and taken 79% of the time.
2. **The doctrine tag never said "pawn-heavy".** It needed more pawns than cards in the Act; AI sides field about 4–9 pawns and play 9–16 cards per Act, so "gambit-heavy" always fired and pawn relics never got their tag.
3. **Some relics had tags no side ever carries** (Strong Square, Battery, Standing Order and Crown Jewel only matched Opening schools).
