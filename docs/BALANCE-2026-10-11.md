# Balance pass (2026-10-11)

Overnight balance pass on the chess variant (branch `test-product-2`). Linked from [SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md](SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md) (dated specs and reports). Targets are in [CHESS.md](CHESS.md) "Balance targets" and [RELICS.md](RELICS.md) "Tuning targets and tests".

## BLUF

- **White/Black bias: root cause found and fixed.** The Mid pawnling wave of the "leading" team spawned 292 units ahead, and the leader alternated by wave number starting with **Black on wave 1** of every match. Black's first Mid wave reached the clash first, so Black led after Act 1 in about 3 of 4 matches. `waves.midLeadUnits` is now 0 (both Mid waves start level, like the side lanes).
- **White win rate now on target.** Relics off: 49.6% (800, seed 29000; before 45.8%) and 51.4% (553 matches, seed 31000, stopped at the 09:10 deadline; all changes in). Both sides drafting relics by AI: 50.4% (800, seed 29000) and 51.1% (800, seed 31000); before 37.5% (40 matches). Median match 14.0–14.3 min (target 12–16).
- **Relics no longer favour Black and no relic is an auto-pick.** The AI's tailored bonus was halved, its "pawn-heavy" tag now fires, and four relics got tags: every relic is picked 14–55% of the time it is offered (Sortie 78.7% → 33–34%; Pawn Structure 0% → 31.6%). Four held-win outliers got one number each and moved into 50–54%.
- **Styles: partial.** Shadow and Regent moved toward parity; Errant, Battering Ram, Usurper (high) and Lancer (low) did not respond to the first numbers and need different levers (below).

## Method

- Full matches: `npm run balance -- --matches N --seed S --quiet --workers 2` with `--relics A=none,B=none` (relics off) or `A=ai,B=ai` (both draft). `--tag NAME` (new) keeps runs from overwriting each other's `out/` files. Long runs were streamed (one `tools/balance-worker.ts` per worker writing one JSON line per match) and merged with `tools/merge-runs.ts` (new), so a run can be read before it ends.
- Act 1 probe (diagnosis only, not committed): the same AI-vs-AI match stepped for one Act (4800 ticks), relics off, recording each side's net worth. Black minus White net worth at the end of Act 1 is a continuous number, so it shows a bias with about 100 matches instead of 800.
- The machine was shared with browser tests (load 25–45 on 4 cores); runs were capped at 2 worker processes, one at a time.

## Root cause: the Mid wave stagger

`spawnWaves` (`src/sim/systems.ts`) spawns every lane's wave for both teams at the base. To stop the short Mid lane from clashing at the exact centre every wave, one team's Mid wave spawned `waves.midLeadUnits` (292.5 on the 1950 map, about 8 s of walking) up the lane. The leader alternated by wave number: `waveNo % 2 === 0 ? 'A' : 'B'`, and the first wave of a match has `waveNo` 1, so **Black always led the first Mid wave**. That first clash decided early last hits, the first Mid trade and the first Tempo, and the lead snowballed: Black was ahead in net worth after Act 1 in 73% of matches, ahead in all three lanes (the Mid piece rotates), and White won about 45%.

Checks that ruled out other causes: the map, bases, towers, slots, obelisks and keeper spots mirror across y = x (`tests/symmetry.test.ts`); jungle shops are point-mirrored but both teams' lane plans are drawn the same way; gambits off kept the bias; reversing the unit update order roughly halved it while the stagger was in place but changed nothing once the stagger was gone.

Fix: `waves.midLeadUnits` 292.5 → 0 in `content/tuning.json` (numbers only; the alternation code stays but has no effect). Both Mid waves now start level and meet at the centre, as the side lanes always did.

## Relics

### Why the AI's picks were lopsided

The AI scores each offer: base 1, +1.0 for the tailored offer when its worst bad callout has severity ≥ 0.6 (nearly every break), +0.5 per matching tag, + jitter 0–0.4. Three things skewed it:

1. **The tailored bonus was decisive.** A trailing side's tailored slot is a Rally relic; with a lost Bastion the family is Siege, whose only Rally relic is Sortie, so Sortie was offered most often and taken 79% of the time.
2. **The doctrine tag never said "pawn-heavy".** It needed more pawns than cards in the Act; AI sides field about 4–9 pawns and play 9–16 cards per Act, so "gambit-heavy" always fired and pawn relics never got their tag.
3. **Some relics had tags no side ever carries** (Strong Square, Battery, Standing Order and Crown Jewel only matched Opening schools).

### Findings

- **Rational pieces die half as often, which slowed matches.** With the retreat line, bad-odds flight and dive check on, kills fell from 55 to about 30 per match and the median match grew to 24–29 min in probes; Throne kills, not Bastions, were the bottleneck (Bastion and structure-health levers barely moved length). Ablations (12–24 matches each) showed no single rule was responsible: low-health flight plus walking home accounted for most of the drop, the dive check for some. Lower retreat lines (10–18% by role instead of 20–40%), a weaker Throne and longer respawns brought length back; base heal, Bastion health, structure damage and a busy-structure discount did not.
- **White bias from the sweep order.** The unit sweep always walked forward from `tick % n`, so White's units (made first) acted before Black's in nearly every tick. Once hits nudged victims mid-sweep and pieces judged each other's motion, that gave White 58.8% (S1). Reversing the sweep flipped the piece-death gap (White −61 → +46 of about 800 deaths); alternating the direction every tick, queuing knockback until the collision pass and reading a target's state instead of its motion removed it (S2: 51.3%).
- **Compute** about 0.38–0.39 ms per tick in the final runs, as before (S0 0.391).
- **Where it stands:** two-seed average White 51.4%, median 16.1 min (each seed sits at one edge: 52.3% on 31000, 16.5 min on 29000; repeat runs of the same build moved by about 0.5 min and 3 points). If the owner wants matches clearly under 16 min, the next lever is Throne health 3800 → 3500 (Bastion health, structure damage and base heal did not move length in probes).

## Recommended, not done

- **Styles, second pass (one number each, then 800 × 2 seeds):** Battering Ram: Sapper armor strip −15 → −10 (the strip, not his Blade damage, drives his win rate). Errant: Questing Blow cooldown 11 → 14 s. Usurper: Claimed Crowns heal 20% → 12% max health. Lancer: Lance Through 70 → 90 base damage. Shadow: Creeping Night burn 6 + 12% → 4 + 8% Soul.
- **Relics:** a third seed before touching Wing Gambit (73.6% held win in R3, 53.7% in R2), Royal Keep (64.1% / 51.1%), Strong Square (36.4% / 43.3%). The value targets in RELICS.md were not measured: run `--relics A=ai,B=none`, `A=none,B=none` and `A=random,B=none` at 800 to check "+6–12 points for the drafting side" and "AI rule beats random by ≥ 3".
- **Act 1 lead on seed 31000:** White led after Act 1 in 440 of 800 R3 matches (55%) while winning 51.1%; seed 29000 was 403/397 in both runs and the one-Act probe is neutral (−159 ± 201). The relics-off run on the same seeds (R4) shows the same tilt (300 / 253 after Act 1) with White winning 51.4%, while seed 29000 is 403 / 397 and the one-Act probe is neutral, so it looks like a seed effect; worth one more probe on seeds 31000+ before reading anything into it.
- **Tests under load:** `tests/terrain.test.ts` (60 s) and `tests/lane-share.test.ts` (120 s) have their own timeouts that ignore `--testTimeout` and fail when the machine is busy; they pass when run alone.
- **Unit update order:** `stepUnits` always starts the sweep at `tick % n` and walks forward, so a team whose units sit earlier in the array tends to act first in a tick. It measured as harmless once the Mid stagger was gone (−141 ± 215 vs −159 ± 201 with a randomised direction), so it was left alone. **Done later the same day:** with the combat spacing and judgement change it was worth 58.8% for White, so the sweep now alternates direction every tick (see "Combat spacing and AI judgement").
