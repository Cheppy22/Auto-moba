## 2026-10-11

- **Combat spacing and AI judgement** (branch `test-product-2`; rules in [CHESS.md](CHESS.md) "Combat spacing and AI judgement", runs in [BALANCE-2026-10-11.md](BALANCE-2026-10-11.md)). Why: Cheppy reported fights as one unreadable blob, and pieces chasing into Bastions at low health, taking 2v1s and not running when hurt. **Spacing:** attackers take their own slot on a ring around their target (melee just inside reach, ranged at an 85% standoff), fanned toward their own side so two sides form two lines; teammates keep extra personal space while fighting; heavy piece hits knock the victim back up to 6 units (queued, applied with collisions, never through walls; `damage.kb`); path followers route around walls back to their path. **Judgement:** retreat line by role (King 15%, Queen 12%, Rook 10%, Bishop 18%, Knight 12%) with a 12-point margin to turn back; no fight below 0.45 local power ratio (an even 2v1) unless the kill is sure; no tower dives unless healthy, the structure is busy with pawns and the kill is sure; flights last at least 2 s; outmatched healthy pieces fall back to the nearest own structure of their lane; hurt enemy pieces are run down further; pushes start when enemy pieces are away healing. `SnapUnit.engage` added. **Fairness:** the unit sweep now alternates direction each tick (the forward-only sweep let White act first and, with the new rules, won White 58.8%). **Pacing:** pieces now die about half as often (28 kills per match, was 55), so the Throne has 3800 health (was 6800) and respawns start at 18 s + 6 s per Act (was 10 + 4, cap 70 s, was 60). Effect (800 × 2 seeds, relics AI): White 53.0% / 49.9% (seeds 29000 / 31000; kills even per side), median 15.6 / 15.1 min (before 50.8% / 14.9 at seed 29000). Blob share 66% → 19%, piece deaths in tower range 5.5 → 2.6 per match, "outnumbered" deaths 5.1 → 2.0, stuck units over 200 matches 5 → 0, hero damage in Mid 25% → 38%. New tool `npm run combat-check`; determinism baseline re-recorded.

## Notes carried from the original build (still true)

- No projectiles and no collision: attacks and abilities resolve instantly, and units may overlap.
- Abilities never target structures; only auto-attacks damage towers and guardians.
- Unit iteration order rotates each tick, and units with a lethal hit pending still act that tick (otherwise the first unit always won simultaneous kills).
- Numbers mostly live in `content/tuning.json`; AI scoring weights in `src/sim/ai/strategic.ts` and `behavior.ts` are mostly inline.
- Determinism: the same seed gives an identical event log; replay export reproduces a match exactly.
