# Long Board balance, phase 4 (2026-10-12)

Phase 4 of the Long Board ([MAP-LONGBOARD-2026-10-11.md](MAP-LONGBOARD-2026-10-11.md)): stronger high ground, line of sight at the groves, and an 800-match balance on `?map=long`. Branch `test-product-2`. Linked from [SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md](SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md); rules in [CHESS.md](CHESS.md) "The Long Board". The square map is unchanged (`npm run baseline` identical).

## BLUF

- **High ground now pays.** The higher piece deals 58.9% of the piece damage dealt across tiers (was 48.8%; owner target 55–62%). Three map-only numbers: Reach +30% range (was +15%), a new **Slope** rule (a hit on a target a tier below deals 20% more, a tier above 20% less) and uphill fight odds +20% (was +10%).
- **Line of sight at the groves.** Grove edges block sight and shots except at openings (gates, River Road, Sally spur, the Pit seam). Verge farming is gone; no measurable compute cost.
- **Balance on target.** White 49.2% (seed 29000) and 51.8% (seed 31000), median match 14.8 / 14.8 min, 0 stuck units in 100 matches (longest stall 1 s; was 4.4 s). First Bastion still about 2.2 min (left as the owner asked).

## Method

- Full matches: `npm run balance -- --map long --matches 800 --seed S --quiet --workers 2` (both sides draft relics by AI, the usual run), seeds 29000 and 31000, one job at a time on a shared 4-core machine.
- Cross-level damage, stuck units and spacing: `npm run combat-check -- --map long --matches N --seed 29000` (the final 100 ran as two halves of 50, seeds 29000 and 29050, summed). Tuning probes used 40 matches (the high-ground share moves about ±2 points at that size); before and after use 100.
- "Cross-level" = piece-on-piece damage where attacker and target stand on different tiers (a ramp counts as its lower tier); "higher share" = the part of it dealt by the piece on the higher tier. Tiers are read after the tick the hit lands in.

The fight-odds bonus (pieces take fewer uphill fights) and the longer Reach moved it the rest of the way. The chosen set keeps each rule to one sentence and leaves Sight unchanged.

## Line of sight

- Rule: a straight line between two units may cross a grove's edge only where walkable ground carries on outside it (an opening). Implemented in `lineOfSight` (`src/sim/world/height.ts`): only lines that touch a grove's bounding box are checked, sampled every 10 units, the edge found by bisection and tested with `walkable`. Deterministic.
- Used by: attack reach (`inAttackRange`), every target scan (pieces, pawns, pawnlings, camps, Bastions and Thrones, event units), skill targeting (an area skill hits only what its centre can see) and the AI's sure-kill check. A unit without a line of sight to its target walks at the target, around the edge to an opening.
- Verge farming before the change (4 matches, seed 29000): about 0.14% of all damage with a piece involved crossed a grove edge outside an opening (piece hits and skills on grove camps, and some piece duels across the edge). After: 0.01% (skill side effects and item procs).
- Compute: 0.88 ms per tick against 0.92 ms before on the same 4 seeds (CPU time, noise on a loaded machine); the 800-match runs logged 0.743 and 0.698 ms per tick (wall clock, shared machine).
- The Pit has no line-of-sight rule: Mid and the River Road run through it and it lies on the river's own low tier, so there is nothing to look past.

## Other change

- A piece no longer picks a target it would neither hit nor chase. Sight (looking down a tier) spots farther than a piece's chase leash; such a pick stopped a Rook at a grove gate for 11 s in one 40-match probe. Maps with heights only.
