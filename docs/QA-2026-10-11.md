## Product review

**What is fun**

- The Act rhythm: countdown pulse, pending grey, three tolls, halt, break sheet, Resume. It gives the match a heartbeat and a natural point to think.
- The Act report reads like a coach: "You lost a Bastion on the Left", "Your Right Knight died 4×", lanes at the bell, how pieces fell. It is the best screen in the game.
- Relics with a tailored first card, "Offered because…", and "Let the AI choose" everywhere. Black's visible gambit banners ("Black played Check on your Bishop") make Black feel like a player.
- Forks are short, clear and pause the game; the style emblems and one-line descriptions are enough to choose.
- The table look is a big step up in charm and readability (felt lanes, brass edges, walnut frame); the intro card (Openings, both courts) sets the stage well.
- Matches are short (6–17 minutes) and the White side's auto pawn plan means an idle player still gets a game.

**What is confusing**

- The score ("0 / 13") never says what it counts, and White can win while the gold chart shows Black ahead the whole match: nothing explains why.
- Relic cards are dense text with no picture of what they touch; Sealed Move is two dropdowns, Battery moves your pieces when taken.
- The first minutes give no guidance: Tempo, Field Pawn and the three-card hand are explained only by their labels.
- Several things happen to you without a message: Sealed Move (fixed), Standing Order, Pawn Structure and the pawn plan fielding pawns, a Rally relic switching on and off.
- On a phone the default look shows pieces as specks and the table look wastes the screen height; the board reads through the rosters.

**Top 5 improvements by impact**

1. Finish the table look: lane lean-in (Q8), fit the board to portrait height (Q9), then make it the default. It fixes the biggest readability complaint.
2. Say what the score is ("Captures" or "Points") and add three one-time coach tips (Begin, first Tempo 50, first break). Turn Opening and paths into "More options" on the first match, and give the setup a scroll cue (Q11).
3. Put a small situation board at the top of the Act Report (three lanes, Bastions standing, where pieces fell) and turn callouts into buttons ("Move Knight to Left", "See relics for this"). Merge Orders and relic aiming into one Plan tab.
4. One notice band at a time (Q12), and a message whenever the game acts for you (pawn plan, Standing Order, Pawn Structure, Rally on or off).
5. Sound and feel: the bell and clack exist; add a capture moment (a piece tipping over) and make the tolls and Check louder than everything else. Also fix the tailored relic mapping so the first card answers the problem (Q10).
