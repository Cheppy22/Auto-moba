# Map spec: "The Long Board" (2026-10-11)

Design spec for the owner's workshop decision (2026-10-11). Status: **v2 approved by Cheppy 2026-10-11 (decisions in section 6); phases 1–2 (sim), 3 (table render and camera) and 4 (high ground, grove line of sight, balance: [BALANCE-2026-10-12.md](BALANCE-2026-10-12.md)) built 2026-10-12; phase 5 (the default map, full screen, free camera, Grand Board look retired) done 2026-10-12. Rules and view in [CHESS.md](CHESS.md) "The Long Board"**. v2 applies the owner's sketch on v1: groves turned 180°, verges, Sally Paths. Branch `test-product-2`. Diagram: [img/map-longboard.svg](img/map-longboard.svg).

## 1. BLUF

1. **Two 8×8 chessboards end to end:** a 2000 × 4000 board. White's base is at the bottom, Black's at the top. Three straight lane-files (Left, Mid, Right) run base to base, with a river on the seam.
2. **Walks are about 1.5× longer:** the average lane goes from 2611 to 3839 units (×1.47). Raising unit speed by 15% (`speedMul` 1.5 → 1.725, owner decision) makes walks take about 25% longer in time, and should keep the median match in 12–16 min (predicted 14.5–15.5).
3. **Three heights:** low river, a midfield terrace and a high home terrace, joined by 12 narrow lane ramps. There are two simple rules: **Reach** (ranged units shooting down get +15% range) and **Sight** (units on high ground spot enemies below 25% farther, and units below only see them up close).
4. **Five big zones instead of 13 scattered spots:** two groves placed diagonally (each team's grove sits in its own half, on its own Right, and reaches the river), the Pit (Keeper, obelisks, neutral events) and one shop Square per side. A walkable **verge** runs between each side lane and its grove. In the empty corner opposite each grove, a winding **Sally Path** links your Left lane to your Square and has a spur to the tip of the enemy grove. The River Road crosses the middle. 57% of the board is open ground (40% today).
5. **Fairness is now a 180° turn about the Pit** (it was a mirror across the river). Both teams get exactly the same board from their own seat. The lane ids stay the same, but White's Left lane is Black's Right lane.
6. **Effort:** about 9–12 agent-days across 5 phases. Building it behind `?map=long` takes about 5–6 calendar days. Biggest risks: AI rotation/pathing, and the old Grand Board look (recommend retiring it).

## 2. Dimensions and coordinates

Sim units. x runs across (0 = White's left edge), and y runs along the board (0 = Black's end, 4000 = White's end). One chess square is 250. **Fairness: a 180° turn about the Pit**, (x, y) → (2000 − x, 4000 − y), swaps the teams. Every zone, spot and lane has a twin (West ↔ East, `top` ↔ `bot`); only Mid and the Pit map onto themselves. Lane ids stay `top` / `mid` / `bot`, named from White's seat (Left / Mid / Right). Seen from Black's own seat, `bot` is its Left and `top` is its Right. Black's coordinates below are White's turned 180°. Below, `e = |y − 2000|` is the distance from the river.

**What the turn means for lanes and the 2/1/2 split.** The split itself is unchanged (two pieces Left, one Mid, two Right). Each side lane is no longer the same at both ends, though. In White's half, the Left lane (`top`) has White's Sally Path; in Black's half it runs past Black's grove. So every team has the same two kinds of side lane, from its own seat:

- **Grove side (own Right):** your farm and your verge are beside it.
- **Route side (own Left):** your Sally Path is near home, and the enemy grove's river tip is up ahead.

White in `top` is in exactly the same spot as Black in `bot`. Any code that names a lane id (AI lane picks, tie-breaks in `LANES` order, setup notes, Black's AI setup) must think in the team's own seat, or the turn stops being fair.

## 3. High ground

How the sim stores height: a new pure module `src/sim/world/height.ts` with `tierAt(world, x, y): 0 | 1 | 2`. It returns 0 inside a grove, the Pit or a grove gate (verges and Sally Paths follow `e`). Everywhere else it is 0 when e < 370, 1 when e < 1120, and 2 otherwise. The result is cached per 25-unit terrain cell in a `Uint8Array` that is built once in `buildWorld`. It uses integer comparisons only, so it is deterministic. The table view's `Relief.areaAt` / `atSim` read the same tiers (they stop computing their own) and add visual-only offsets: the Pit −18, a dais on the base, and the faces of the terraces.

| Rule      | Exact rule (numbers in `tuning.height`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Feel                                                                                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reach** | When the attacker's tier is above the target's, its attack range is ×1.15 (`reachMul`). Applies only to attackers with base range ≥ 60 (`reachMinRange`): Queen 80 → 92, Bishop 110 → 126, ranged pawnlings 85 → 98. Melee, skills' own areas and **structures** are unchanged. | Ranged pieces hold the top of a ramp and poke down.                                         |
| **Sight** | Choosing a target (`behavior.ts`, the aggro scan): looking down, the radius is `aggroRadius` × 1.25 (150 → 187). Looking up, a unit only picks targets within its attack range + 20. Same tier: unchanged.                                                                      | High ground engages first. Units below get "jumped" at the ramp foot.                       |
| **Ramps** | Lanes narrow to 130 wide for 120 units at each terrace edge. No speed penalty, so walk-time maths stays simple.                                                                                                                                                                 | Natural chokepoints. A Barricade on a ramp is 154 wide instead of 204 and seals it cleanly. |

**Counterplay.** Bonuses vanish as soon as you stand on the same tier, so climb behind your pawnling wave: it soaks the first shots. Melee pieces (King, Rook, Knight) ignore Reach. The Knight's leap already clears solid terrain, so it can jump a terrace edge instead of taking the ramp. Area gambits (Sanctuary, Pawn Storm, Siege) ignore tiers. A Barricade on a ramp forces an attacker onto the River Road, a grove or the Sally Path. The verges look down into the groves, so ranged pieces on a verge get Reach on camps and on invaders. Counter that by entering through the Mid gate or the Sally spur. Bastions get no bonus, so tower balance is unchanged.

**How the AI uses it** (small, local rules):

1. **Hold the top.** A piece with posture `defend`, or one whose lane front is behind its outer Bastion, stops at a hold point 60 units above the nearest ramp top in its lane rather than walking down to meet the wave.
2. **Climb with the wave.** An attacking piece waits at the ramp foot until its own pawnlings are on the ramp. This extends the existing siege gather (`siegeGather`, `siegeWaitTicks`).
3. **Fight odds.** `matchupAt` (`ai/power.ts`) gives the side holding the higher tier at the fight centre +10% power, so the AI picks fights uphill less often.
4. **Ranged kite.** Queen and Bishop, while lane-farming, step back up the ramp when a melee threat closes. This reuses the existing retreat path, with the target point set at the ramp top.
5. **Own seat.** Lane choices and tie-breaks use the team's own Left / Right (Black's `LANES` order reversed). The jungler farms its near grove first; it invades the enemy river tip through its Sally spur only in Act 3, and only when it holds the fight odds.

Predicted effect: longer walk-outs (+3–5 s per trip) and high-ground defence lengthen matches; a costlier death speeds up snowballs. Net estimate is a 14.5–15.5 min median. **If the median is above 15.5:** set `tower.hp` 3600 → 3300 first, then `speedMul` 1.8. **If it is below 12.5:** set `speedMul` 1.65. Watch **Open File** (+25% speed for 15 s is worth more on a long lane) and Barricade (ramps make it stronger).

**What to measure** (`npm run balance -- --matches 800 --seed 29000 --quiet --workers 2`, relics off and both-AI; then seed 31000):

- White win rate 48–52%; median match plus p10 / p90 minutes.
- When the first Bastion falls, per lane, and Act-1 net-worth lead (vs today).
- Fight share by tier, and the high-ground side's fight win rate. Target 55–62%: an edge, not a lock.
- Stuck units: no progress for 20 s while out of combat. Must be 0.
- Turn fairness: win rate of the grove side vs the route side, per lane and per team (should match for White and Black); invasions via the Sally spur and river-tip camps taken by the enemy.
- Lane share (`tests/lane-share.test.ts`), camps cleared per Act, Keeper buys, obelisk claims per side, Barricades placed on ramps, Open File impact, style pick rates (≥ 15%).

## 6. Owner decisions (2026-10-11)

1. **Unit speed +15%** (`speedMul` 1.5 → 1.725; walks take about 1.28× as long).
2. **Retire the Grand Board look** (no port to the new map; delete it when the Long Board becomes default).
3. **Both high-ground rules (Reach and Sight)**, weakened if the high-ground side wins over 62% of fights — **plus a minimalist indicator**: a small eye icon above a unit's head while it has the high-ground edge (Sight/Reach active), table-look style, readable at phone scale, not cluttering fights.
4. **Clearing order:** home ends first (Act 1 W1 + E1), middles Act 2, river tips Act 3.
5. **Keep the Sally Path spur** into the enemy grove's river tip.
6. **Build behind `?map=long`** until the balance runs pass, then flip the default.
