**Why the card player stays near 50%:** in every configuration tried (cards ×1.5 to ×3 of the 2026-10-14 numbers, full price or a fifth cheaper, Black's AI card share 13%, 11%, 9% or 7%) the card-a-minute stand-in won 47–53% on the same seeds. The pawn plan's lane quotas leave Tempo unspent, so a card's price does not limit the player; a stronger card helps Black's AI as much as the stand-in, which plays the AI's own best-fitting card once a minute. What the changes did move is the idle player (36–48%). A next step would change what a player can do that the AI does not: cards that only the player is dealt, a hand-played card cheaper than the AI's, or a stand-in that plays at the moments a person would.
